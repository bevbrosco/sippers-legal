Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Sippers ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use the Sippers mobile application (the "Service"), and explains your privacy rights and how the law protects you.

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Definitions

  • Account means a unique account created for you to access the Service.
  • Application or Service refers to Sippers, the mobile software provided by the Company.
  • Company (referred to as "the Company," "We," "Us," or "Our") refers to Sippers, operating from Massachusetts, United States.
  • Personal Data means any information that relates to an identified or identifiable individual.
  • Service Provider means a third party that processes data on our behalf.
  • You means the individual using the Service.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

When you create an account and use Sippers, you provide:

  • Email address — used for authentication via one-time passcode (OTP), Google Sign-In, or Sign in with Apple.
  • Username and display name — public identifiers shown on your profile and reviews.
  • Name (optional) — used to personalize your profile if you choose to provide one. May be displayed to other users.
  • Date of birth — used to verify you meet the minimum age requirement (18+). Stored but never displayed publicly.
  • Profile avatar (optional) — image you upload for your profile.
  • Reviews and ratings — written reviews, numerical ratings, and review photos you create.
  • Social interactions — follows, follow requests, and blocks.

Information Collected from Your Device

With your permission, the Service may access:

  • Camera — to capture photos for reviews and your profile.
  • Photo library — to select photos for reviews and your profile.
  • Location (optional) — to show beverages and spots near you and to suggest nearby stores, bars, and cafés when you choose to tag where you got a drink. Location is used when you are using these features; we do not track your location in the background.
  • Push notifications (optional) — if you enable notifications, we store a device push token to deliver them. You can turn notifications off at any time in the app or your device settings.

You can enable or disable these permissions at any time in your device settings.

Bot and Abuse Protection

When you request an email sign-in code, Cloudflare Turnstile may evaluate your IP address, browser or device characteristics, and interaction and security signals to help distinguish people from automated traffic. Sippers sends the short-lived challenge token to Supabase to authorize the email-code request. We do not send your email address, beverage history, photos, or other form entries to Turnstile, and Sippers does not retain the challenge token.

Purchase Location Tags (Optional)

When logging a drink, you may optionally tag the place where you bought or drank it (for example, a specific store, bar, or café). A tag stores the place’s name, address, general category, and map coordinates, along with the beverage you logged. Tagging is always optional per log, and you control whether your tagged spots are visible to others through your account’s privacy settings. Place search suggestions are provided by Apple Maps directly from your device.

AI Label Scanning (Optional)

If you affirmatively choose the label-scanning feature, Sippers sends the one or two label photos you select and, when available, the product barcode to Anthropic’s commercial API. Anthropic analyzes the images as untrusted product-label content and returns extracted product information, such as the beverage name, brand, ingredients, nutrition facts, and caffeine content. We do not send your profile, account history, or other photos with this request. You can skip this feature and enter product information manually.

The extracted result, the model and provider used, and processing timestamps may be stored with the beverage record for app operation, troubleshooting, and audit purposes. If you continue and choose a label photo as the beverage photo, that photo is uploaded and handled like other user-submitted content.

Crash and Diagnostic Data

To diagnose and fix bugs, we use Sentry to collect crash reports and error logs. This may include device type, operating system version, app version, error stack traces, and a pseudonymous account identifier. We do not use Sentry or any other tool for behavioral analytics or marketing.

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and operate the Service — authenticate you, manage your account, display your reviews and profile, and enable social features.
  • Verify your eligibility — confirm that you meet the minimum age requirement (18+).
  • Communicate with you — send authentication codes, account-related security notices, and respond to your support requests. We do not send marketing emails.
  • Diagnose technical issues — investigate crashes and errors to improve reliability.
  • Enforce our Terms — review reports, enforce content rules, and protect against abuse.
  • Comply with legal obligations — respond to lawful requests from authorities.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell your personal information. (We do license aggregated, de-identified market insights that cannot be used to identify you — see “Aggregated Beverage Insights” below.)
  • We do not use behavioral or marketing analytics.
  • We do not show advertisements or share your data with ad networks.
  • We do not use cookies or web tracking (Sippers is a native mobile app).
  • We do not send marketing emails, newsletters, or promotional messages.
  • We do not use your personal information or content to train our own artificial intelligence or machine learning models. Anthropic states that commercial API inputs and outputs are not used for model training by default unless a customer submits feedback or opts in; we do not submit your label scans as feedback or opt them into training.

Third-Party Service Providers

We use the following third-party services to operate Sippers. Each is bound by its own privacy policy, linked below:

  • Supabase — hosts our database, authentication, and server-side functions. It receives account information, app content and metadata, and references to uploaded files. Legacy files may also remain in Supabase storage until they are deleted or migrated. Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare — hosts current user-uploaded avatars, review photos, and sip photos in R2 object storage and provides Turnstile bot and abuse protection for email authentication. Turnstile may process the security signals described above and provide challenge analytics to Sippers. Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic — processes the optional label photos and barcode described above and returns extracted product information. Privacy Policy
  • Sentry — collects crash and error reports. Privacy Policy
  • Google — provides Google Sign-In as an authentication option. Privacy Policy
  • Apple — provides Sign in with Apple as an authentication option. Privacy Policy
  • Product databases — we query Open Food Facts, USDA FoodData Central, and FatSecret for product barcode and nutrition lookups. We send only product barcodes; no personal data is sent.
  • Expo — delivers push notifications if you enable them. Receives your device push token. Privacy Policy
  • Apple Maps — provides place search when you tag a purchase location. Search queries go from your device to Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.

If you set your account to private, your profile and content are visible only to your approved followers. Direct identifiers like your email address and date of birth are never public.
  • Service Providers — we share data with the providers listed above as needed to operate the Service.
  • Legal compliance — we may disclose information if required by law, court order, or valid government request, or to protect rights, property, safety, or to prevent fraud or abuse.
  • Business transfers — if we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred. We will notify you and ensure protections continue.
  • With your consent — for any other purpose with your explicit consent.

Aggregated Beverage Insights

Like other beverage community platforms, we create aggregated, de-identified insights from activity on the Service — for example, which beverages are trending, how a product is rated, or where a category is popular by retail chain, region, and week. We may use these insights ourselves and may license or sell them to third parties such as beverage brands, retailers, distributors, and market-research firms.

These insights are statistics about beverages and places, not about you:

  • They never include your name, username, email address, or any other identifier.
  • They are only produced at a group level — a statistic is included only when it reflects the activity of multiple users, so no data point can be traced back to an individual.
  • We maintain this data in de-identified form, we do not attempt to re-identify it, and we contractually require recipients not to attempt re-identification.

Your personal information itself is never sold or shared with data brokers or advertisers. Tagging where you bought a drink — the main input to place-based insights — is always optional.

Sharing to External Platforms

Sippers includes a feature that lets you share images of your profile or beverage activity to other apps and services (such as Instagram, Messages, or social platforms) through your device's share menu. The image you create may include your username, display name, profile photo, and beverage statistics. Sharing is initiated entirely by you; we do not transmit data to external services automatically. Once you share content to another platform, it is governed by that platform's own policies.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account from within the app (Settings → Delete Account), we permanently remove your account, profile, reviews, and uploaded photos from our active systems.

Anthropic states that standard commercial API inputs and outputs are automatically deleted from its backend within 30 days, subject to exceptions for services configured for longer retention, agreed retention terms, usage-policy enforcement, and legal requirements. Sippers may separately retain the extracted product information as described above.

Sippers does not retain Cloudflare Turnstile challenge tokens. Cloudflare may retain security and challenge records under its applicable service terms and privacy documentation.

Reviews that have been reported and are subject to ongoing moderation may be retained as moderation evidence even after a delete request, in accordance with our enforcement responsibilities. Crash and error reports collected by Sentry are retained per Sentry's standard retention policy. Backup copies may persist in encrypted backups for a limited period for disaster recovery.

We may retain certain information longer where required by legal obligation, to defend legal claims, or to address security or fraud issues.

We may also create aggregated, de-identified, or anonymized data from personal information by removing or modifying details that identify you. Such data is not personal information, and we may use, retain, and disclose it for any lawful purpose, including analytics, research, improving the Service, and the licensed insights described in “Aggregated Beverage Insights” above. Aggregated data may be retained after you delete your account, since it does not identify you. We do not attempt to re-identify aggregated or anonymized data.

Your Privacy Rights

Regardless of where you live, you have the following rights with respect to your personal information:

  • Right to access — request a copy of the personal information we hold about you. You can download a portable JSON copy of data controlled directly by Sippers from Settings → Export My Data.
  • Right to correct — update inaccurate or incomplete information. Name, username, avatar, birthday, account visibility, caffeine display, and notification preferences can be corrected directly in the app, subject to safety rules such as the 18+ minimum and username-change cooldown.
  • Right to delete — request deletion of your account and personal information. You can do this directly in the app at Settings → Delete Account.
  • Right to portability — request a copy of your data in a portable format. The in-app JSON export covers data controlled directly by the Sippers application; contact us for a broader request involving provider-held records.
  • Right to object or restrict — object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Right to withdraw consent — withdraw any consent you have previously provided. AI label-photo permission is requested separately for every scan, and choosing Cancel sends nothing. Device camera, photo-library, location, and notification permissions can be changed in your device settings.

To exercise a right that is not available in the app, contact us from the email address tied to your account at bevbrosco@gmail.com with the subject PRIVACY REQUEST, the type of request, and your Sippers username. Do not send your password, a one-time code, government ID, or a copy of your data export. We use the existing account/email channel to verify requests when possible and request only additional information that is reasonably necessary. We will respond within the period required by applicable law and will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.
